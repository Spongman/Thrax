The MOS 6502 target offers two CPU models in Settings: `nmos6502` (the existing
default) and `w65c02` (WDC W65C02S). They share the assembler engine, parser,
CPU execution switch, memory/device model, debugger controller, history codec,
editor providers and examples. The selected model binds explicit encoding,
addressing and timing tables; it does not add another architecture target.

W65C02S adds BRA, STZ, TRB, TSB, PHX/PLX, PHY/PLY, accumulator INC/DEC,
JMP (absolute,X), zero-page indirect ALU/load/store operations, immediate and
indexed BIT, RMB0..7, SMB0..7, BBR0..7, BBS0..7, WAI and STP. All documented
reserved NOP encodings have their WDC byte lengths and cycle counts; `.byte`
can emit them, while source `NOP` keeps the canonical $EA encoding. NMOS
assembly rejects CMOS instructions/addressing forms and supports the NMOS
undocumented instructions in those byte slots. CMOS never inherits NMOS
additions: for example $07 is SLO/RMB0, $CB is AXS/WAI, and $DB is DCP/STP.
See [NMOS notes](NMOS.md) for aliases and unstable-opcode behavior.

CMOS JMP (indirect) increments across a pointer page boundary. Decimal ADC/SBC
sets N/Z from the adjusted result and takes one extra cycle. Interrupts and BRK
push the previous D flag before clearing it; RTI restores it. Absolute-indexed
read/modify/write takes six cycles in the same page, seven across a page.

WAI advances PC and retires once, then suspends fetch. The workbench timer
continues to the next enabled timer event without busy polling. IRQ wakes WAI
even while I is set (resuming without vectoring); unmasked IRQ/NMI vector
normally. Without an interrupt source the run pauses waiting, preserving WAI
state, and Continue retries it. STP stops until reset; explicit debugger edits
to PC and rewind may also leave the stop. WAI/STP state, device changes,
elapsed waiting cycles and interrupt transitions are reversible through the
same typed history schema/effects as the NMOS machine.

This remains a CPU teaching machine with THRAX's existing console, keyboard
and timer at $F000, not a WDC development board. Pin-level RDY, bus arbitration,
dummy reads/writes and electrical clock behavior are not simulated. Registers
receive the existing deterministic debugger reset values, rather than hardware
power-on indeterminacy. Decimal arithmetic is intended for valid BCD digits.

Authoritative encoding, timing and operational differences: WDC W65C02S
datasheet, April 8 2022, tables 5-2, 6-4 and 7-1:
https://www.westerndesigncenter.com/wdc/documentation/w65c02s.pdf
